## English (U.S.)

**Name**

TokenRemain

**Subtitle**

AI quota, always in view

**Promotional text**

See Mac-sourced AI coding quotas on iPhone, widgets, Live Activities, and Apple
Watch—without creating another account.

**Description**

TokenRemain keeps the AI coding quotas already monitored on your Mac visible
across your Apple devices.

Your Mac remains the only provider-data source. The separately distributed
TokenRemain Mac app reads the tools you choose and creates a privacy-filtered
snapshot. Your iPhone displays that snapshot in a focused dashboard with clear
capture times, real aggregate trends, reset countdowns, and risk projections.

Included with this paid download:

- iPhone overview, limits, and aggregate trend pages
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
- Live Activity and Dynamic Island views
- Apple Watch glance, Smart Stack, and complications
- configurable overview cards and provider expansion
- foreground freshness measurements that distinguish provider capture from
  iCloud receipt

Private by design:

- no TokenRemain account or Sign in with Apple
- no subscription, ads, or in-app purchase
- provider credentials never leave the Mac
- no prompts, projects, conversations, or request-level records are synced
- allowlisted snapshots use AES-256-GCM and the user's CloudKit Private Database
- widgets and Watch receive only an already validated display snapshot

TokenRemain for Mac is required for live provider data. Both devices must use
the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive and iCloud Keychain enabled. Open
TokenRemain once on each device and they connect automatically. Demo Mode is
available to preview every surface without connecting provider data.

Background delivery timing is controlled by Apple. When the iPhone app is
active, TokenRemain combines CloudKit change notifications with foreground
reconciliation; locked, suspended, force-quit, offline, and WidgetKit refresh
states do not have a fixed delivery guarantee.

## Simplified Chinese

**名称**

TokenRemain

**副标题**

AI 编码额度，随时掌握

**宣传文本**

把 Mac 上的 AI 编码额度安全带到 iPhone、小组件、实时活动与 Apple Watch；无需注册新账号。

**描述**

TokenRemain 让你在 Apple 设备上随时查看 Mac 已监测的 AI 编码额度。

Mac 始终是唯一 Provider 数据源。单独下载的 TokenRemain Mac 版读取你选择的本地工具，生成经过隐私白名单过滤的快照；iPhone 以清晰的采集时间、真实聚合趋势、重置倒计时和风险预测呈现这些数据。

本次付费下载包含：

- iPhone 概览、额度与聚合趋势页面
- 主屏幕和锁屏小组件
- 实时活动与灵动岛
- Apple Watch 概览、智能叠放与复杂功能
- 可增删、排序并原地展开 Provider 的概览组件
- 区分 Provider 采集时间与 iCloud 接收时间的前台新鲜度指标

隐私边界：

- 不建立 TokenRemain 账号，不要求 Sign in with Apple
- 无订阅、广告或应用内购买
- Provider 凭证始终留在 Mac
- 不同步提示词、项目、对话或逐请求记录
- 白名单快照使用 AES-256-GCM，并存入用户自己的 CloudKit 私有数据库
- 小组件与 Watch 只接收主 App 已校验的展示快照

查看真实 Provider 数据需要 TokenRemain Mac 版。两台设备需登录同一 iCloud 账户并开启 iCloud Drive 与 iCloud 钥匙串；分别打开一次 TokenRemain 后会自动连接。未连接数据时，可使用带明确标记的演示模式预览全部界面。

iOS 后台时机由系统控制。iPhone App 活跃时，TokenRemain 会结合 CloudKit 变更提示与前台兜底拉取；锁屏、挂起、强退、离线或 WidgetKit 后台刷新不承诺固定到达时间。
